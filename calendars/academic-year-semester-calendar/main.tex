{\large\bfseries\textcolor{inknavy}{About This Calendar}}\\[2mm]
\RaggedRight
\small
This twelve-month wall calendar tracks every academic milestone from New Student
Orientation through the close of Summer Session II. Each month is laid out as a
full grid with color-coded markers for orientation events, exams, breaks, and
key deadlines, followed by a compact list of that month's dates. Post it above
your desk, in a department office, or in a residence hall common room to keep
the whole term visible at a glance.\par
\vspace{3mm}
\small
Dates reflect the official Meridian University Registrar calendar as published
for the 2026--2027 academic year. Individual schools and colleges may observe
additional deadlines; consult your advising office for program-specific dates.
