\section*{\faInfoCircle\ 1. PURPOSE \& SCOPE}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the validated workflow for cell lysis, Immobilized Metal Affinity Chromatography (IMAC) purification, size-exclusion buffer exchange, and continuous spectrophotometric NADH-coupled kinase activity assay of human recombinant Kinase V-321.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Scope:} Applies to all research personnel within Molecular Biochemistry at Meridian Biosciences Inc. executing lot release, stability testing, or kinetic profiling for Project Kinase-V321.
  \item \textbf{Quality Goal:} Maintain lot consistency with protein purity $\ge 95\%$, specific activity $\ge 280\text{ U/mg}$, and assay coefficient of variation ($\text{CV}$) $< 5\%$.
\end{itemize}

% ==========================================
% SECTION 2: SAFETY PRECAUTIONS & HAZARDS
% ==========================================
\section*{\faBiohazard\ 2. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS \& HAZARD CONTROL}

\begin{tcolorbox}[hazardbox]
  \scriptsize
  Strict adherence to Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) practices is mandatory. All operations involving chemical lysis reagents and inhibitors must be conducted with appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=10pt]
    \item \textbf{PMSF (Phenylmethylsulfonyl fluoride):} Highly toxic serine protease inhibitor ($\text{LD}_{50} = 200\text{ mg/kg}$). Causes severe skin burns. Prepare fresh solutions in anhydrous Isopropanol inside a certified chemical fume hood.
    \item \textbf{Acrylamide \& SDS (SDS-PAGE):} Neurotoxin and suspected carcinogen. Handle monomer gels in the fume hood.
    \item \textbf{Imidazole \& Dithiothreitol (DTT):} Corrosive skin irritants. Avoid inhalation of dust or contact with eyes.
    \item \textbf{Required PPE:} Lab coat, safety goggles with side shields, double nitrile gloves, and closed-toe non-porous footwear.
  \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% SECTION 4: REAGENT PREPARATION PROCEDURES
% ==========================================
\section*{\faVial\ 4. REAGENT \& BUFFER PREPARATION}

Prepare all buffers using Milli-Q ultrapure water ($18.2\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$ resistivity at 25$^\circ$C). Filter chromatography buffers through a 0.22 $\mu$m PES membrane prior to use.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt]
  \item \textbf{Lysis Buffer L-1 (500 mL):} 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8.0), 300 mM NaCl, 20 mM Imidazole, 10\% (v/v) Glycerol, 0.1\% (v/v) Triton X-100. Store at 4$^\circ$C. Add fresh 1.0 mM PMSF and 0.5 mg/mL Lysozyme prior to use.
  \item \textbf{Wash Buffer W-1 (500 mL):} 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8.0), 500 mM NaCl, 40 mM Imidazole, 5\% (v/v) Glycerol. Store at 4$^\circ$C.
  \item \textbf{Elution Buffer E-1 (250 mL):} 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8.0), 300 mM NaCl, 300 mM Imidazole, 10\% (v/v) Glycerol. Store at 4$^\circ$C.
  \item \textbf{Kinase Assay Buffer A-1 (5x Stock, 100 mL):} 250 mM HEPES-NaOH (pH 7.5), 50 mM $\text{MgCl}_2$, 5 mM EGTA, 0.05\% (v/v) Tween-20. Aliquot and store at -20$^\circ$C. Supplement with 1 mM DTT on day of assay.
\end{itemize}

% ==========================================
% SECTION 5: STEP-BY-STEP PROTOCOL
% ==========================================
\section*{\faLayerGroup\ 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL}

\subsection*{\faClipboard\ Phase I: Cell Lysis \& Clarification}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Retrieve frozen \textit{E. coli} BL21(DE3) cell pellet (approx. 10.0 g wet weight) from -80$^\circ$C freezer and thaw on ice for 25 min.
  \item Resuspend pellet in 50 mL ice-cold Lysis Buffer L-1 (5 mL/g cell paste) using a magnetic stirrer at 4$^\circ$C for 15 min.
  \item Add 50 $\mu$L of 1000x PMSF stock (100 mM in Isopropanol) for a final concentration of 1.0 mM.
  \item Perform ultrasonic lysis on ice: 10 cycles of 30 s ON, 30 s OFF at 40\% output amplitude. Maintain temperature below 8$^\circ$C.
  \item Centrifuge crude lysate at $24,000 \times g$ for 35 min at 4$^\circ$C. Collect clarified supernatant and filter through a 0.45 $\mu$m syringe filter.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{\faFilter\ Phase II: IMAC Chromatography Purification (Ni-NTA)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Mount a 5 mL Ni-NTA Sepharose column onto ÄKTA pure FPLC. Flush with 5 Column Volumes (CV) Milli-Q water at 2.5 mL/min.
  \item Equilibrate column with 8 CV of Lysis Buffer L-1 at 2.0 mL/min.
  \item Load filtered supernatant onto column at 1.0 mL/min to maximize target binding. Collect flow-through (FT).
  \item Wash column with 10 CV of Lysis Buffer L-1, followed by 8 CV of Wash Buffer W-1 at 2.0 mL/min.
  \item Elute bound Kinase V-321 using 100\% Elution Buffer E-1 over 5 CV at 1.5 mL/min. Collect 1.5 mL fractions. Monitor $\text{A}_{280}$ UV.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{\faSync\ Phase III: Buffer Exchange \& Storage}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Pool peak fractions ($\text{A}_{280} > 0.6\text{ AU}$). Load onto PD-10 desalting column pre-equilibrated with Storage Buffer S-1 (50 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 1 mM DTT, 20\% Glycerol).
  \item Concentrate protein using Amicon Ultra-15 10 kDa MWCO centrifugal unit at $4,000 \times g$ ($4^\circ\text{C}$) to $\sim 2.5\text{ mg/mL}$.
  \item Measure protein concentration by UV spectrophotometry at 280 nm ($\epsilon_{280} = 54,490\text{ M}^{-1}\text{cm}^{-1}$).
  \item Aliquot 50 $\mu$L into cryovials, snap-freeze in liquid nitrogen, and store at -80$^\circ$C.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox]
  \scriptsize\textbf{\color{SopTeal}\faCheckCircle\ CRITICAL HOLDING POINT:} Purified Kinase V-321 aliquots may be stored at -80$^\circ$C for up to 6 months. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles ($>2$ cycles results in $>15\%$ loss of activity).
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection*{\faPlayCircle\ 6.2 Spectrophotometric Measurement Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Preheat SpectraMax M5 microplate reader chamber to $30.0^\circ\text{C}$.
  \item Pipette buffer, substrate, coupling enzymes, and Kinase V-321 into duplicate wells (Table 6.1).

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================
% SECTION 7: CALCULATIONS & FORMULAS
% ==========================================
\section*{\faCalculator\ 7. CALCULATIONS \& MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS}

Calculate the linear reaction rate ($\Delta \text{A}_{340}/\text{min}$) from the initial velocity region (minutes 2 to 7).

\begin{tcolorbox}[formulabox]
  \scriptsize\textbf{\color{SopNavy}\faBalanceScale\ Enzyme Volumetric Activity Formula:}
  \begin{equation*}
    \text{Volumetric Activity (U/mL)} = \left( \frac{\Delta \text{A}_{340}/\text{min}}{\epsilon_{\text{NADH}} \times d} \right) \times \left( \frac{V_{\text{total}}}{V_{\text{enzyme}}} \right) \times D
  \end{equation*}
  \vspace{-8pt}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=10pt]
    \item $\Delta \text{A}_{340}/\text{min}$: Linear rate of absorbance decrease at 340 nm ($\text{min}^{-1}$)
    \item $\epsilon_{\text{NADH}}$: Molar extinction coefficient of NADH ($6.22\text{ mM}^{-1}\text{cm}^{-1}$)
    \item $d$: Optical pathlength for 200 $\mu$L in 96-well plate ($0.588\text{ cm}$)
    \item $V_{\text{total}}$: Total assay volume ($0.200\text{ mL}$) \quad \textbar \quad $V_{\text{enzyme}}$: Enzyme volume added ($0.010\text{ mL}$)
    \item $D$: Sample dilution factor ($D = 20$ for 1.0 $\mu$M stock dilution)
  \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[formulabox]
  \scriptsize\textbf{\color{SopNavy}\faBalanceScale\ Specific Activity Calculation:}
  \begin{equation*}
    \text{Specific Activity (U/mg)} = \frac{\text{Volumetric Activity (U/mL)}}{\text{Protein Concentration (mg/mL)}}
  \end{equation*}
  One Unit (U) catalyzes phosphorylation of 1.0 $\mu$mol STK-1 peptide per minute at $30^\circ\text{C}$ under standard assay conditions.
\end{tcolorbox}
