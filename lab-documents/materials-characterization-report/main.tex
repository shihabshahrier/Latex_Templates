% EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
\begin{tcolorbox}[title={\faInfoCircle\ Executive Summary \& Compliance Declaration}, colframe=brandsecondary, colback=white, left=8pt, right=8pt, top=6pt, bottom=6pt]
\small
Comprehensive physical, microstructural, crystallographic, and mechanical characterizations were executed on additive manufactured titanium alloy ($Ti\text{-}6Al\text{-}4V$) sample lot \texttt{NEX-TI64-B09} submitted by Apex Advanced Materials Corp. Analytical evaluation was carried out in accordance with certified laboratory quality management protocols under ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation.

\textbf{Microstructural Analysis:} High-resolution FE-SEM imaging demonstrated a fully transformed $\alpha+\beta$ Widmanstätten (basket-weave) colony structure encapsulated within prior-$\beta$ columnar grains oriented parallel to the additive build direction ($Z$-axis). The mean $\alpha$-lath thickness was quantified at $1.42 \pm 0.18\,\mu\text{m}$. Porosity fraction was exceptionally low ($0.042\%$), consisting of dispersed gas entrapped micro-pores ($d < 15\,\mu\text{m}$) without un-melted region defects.

\textbf{Phase Quantification:} X-ray diffraction (XRD) Rietveld profile refinement verified equilibrium room-temperature phase volume fractions of $88.2 \pm 0.8\%$ HCP $\alpha$-titanium and $11.8 \pm 0.8\%$ BCC $\beta$-titanium, confirming effective dissolution of non-equilibrium martensitic $\alpha'$ phase during solution treatment and aging.

\textbf{Mechanical Conformance:} Uniaxial tensile testing of 5 machined coupons (\texttt{TC-01} through \texttt{TC-05}) demonstrated mean 0.2\% yield strength of $924.6\text{ MPa}$, ultimate tensile strength of $1008.2\text{ MPa}$, elongation at fracture of $12.8\%$, and area reduction of $31.4\%$. All mechanical metrics comfortably surpass ASTM F1472-20 and ASTM B348 Grade 5 specification thresholds.

\textbf{Final Determination:} Material lot \texttt{NEX-TI64-B09} is formally \textbf{APPROVED} for aerospace flight hardware fabrication under Vertex Structural Program specification \texttt{VTX-2026-0418}.
\end{tcolorbox}

% SECTION 2: SEM MICROSTRUCTURE ANALYSIS
\section{Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) \& Microstructural Characterization}

Microstructural evaluation carried out via field-emission SEM revealed a highly dense matrix featuring epitaxial prior-$\beta$ columnar grain growth aligned with the build direction ($Z$-axis). High-magnification secondary electron imaging highlights a fine $\alpha$-lamellae network interspaced with thin residual $\beta$-phase ribs.
