\section*{\faFile\ Protocol Overview and Scope}

This log documents all husbandry, clinical observation, procedural, and enrichment
activity associated with Protocol IACUC-2026-0447 within the rodent barrier suite of
Building 3. Entries must be completed in real time by the technician performing the
activity and countersigned by the attending veterinarian at the intervals specified in
the approved protocol. No retroactive entries are permitted; corrections must be made
with a single strike-through, initialed and dated.

\begin{infobox}
\textbf{Approved animal population}
\begin{itemize}
\item C57BL/6J mice, male and female, 8--12 weeks at enrollment (n = 48)
\item Sprague-Dawley rats, male, 10--14 weeks at enrollment (n = 20)
\item Housing: individually ventilated caging, 4--5 mice per cage or 2 rats per cage
\item Approved procedures: intraperitoneal injection, tail-vein blood draw, oral gavage,
  terminal perfusion under deep anesthesia
\end{itemize}
\end{infobox}
