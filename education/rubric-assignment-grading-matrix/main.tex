\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=slate!60,boxrule=0.5pt,arc=2pt,left=10pt,right=10pt,top=8pt,bottom=8pt]
\small
Marcus, this is a confident and well-researched essay. Your reading of the governess's testimony
is genuinely persuasive, and the evidence you draw from the frame narrative is exactly the kind of
close attention this assignment rewards --- section 2 is the strongest part of the paper.

The thesis in your introduction is clear, but by the third body paragraph the argument has drifted
toward a related claim about narrative reliability in general rather than the specific mechanism you
opened with; tightening that thread would move Criterion~1 from Proficient to Excellent.

Watch comma splices in your topic sentences (paragraphs 4 and 6) and double-check in-text citation
placement relative to closing punctuation per MLA\,9. Overall: a strong essay with a clear path to an
A-range revision. Office hours are open Tuesdays 1--3\,pm if you would like to talk through the
introduction before the portfolio deadline.\\[4mm]
