% MARKET ANALYSIS
\section*{3. Submarket \& Economic Overview}
\sectionbar

\vspace{1pt}
The subject property is located within the Financial District Submarket of Metro City, representing the region's primary commercial business core. Flight-to-quality trends continue to drive strong tenant demand for modern Class A space across the submarket.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=16pt, itemsep=0.5pt, topsep=0.5pt, label=\color{tealaccent}\tiny\faCheckCircle]
  \item \textbf{Submarket Vacancy:} Overall submarket vacancy stands at 10.8\%, while prime Class A vacancy remains tight at 7.2\%.
  \item \textbf{Market Rent Growth:} Direct asking rents for prime space grew 3.4\% year-over-year to an average of \$38.50/SF NNN.
  \item \textbf{Net Absorption:} Positive 142,000 RSF net absorption recorded in YTD 2025 across Class A office assets.
  \item \textbf{Supply Pipeline:} Limited upcoming construction, with only one 120,000 RSF project scheduled for delivery through Q4 2026.
\end{itemize}
