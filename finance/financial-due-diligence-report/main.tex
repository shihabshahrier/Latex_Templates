\chapter{Executive Summary \& Transaction Architecture}

Meridian Advisory Group was engaged by \textbf{Apex Global Holdings} (``Buyer'') to perform a comprehensive financial due diligence audit of \textbf{Synapse Systems Inc.} (``Target'' or ``Synapse'') in connection with the proposed acquisition of 100\% of the issued share capital. Synapse is a provider of cloud-native workflow automation software and data integration platforms serving mid-tier financial institutions.

\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
  \textbf{\textcolor{NavyBlue}{\faCheckCircle\ Key Audit Summary \& Investment Committee Verdict:}} \\
  Our Quality of Earnings (QoE) analysis confirms an \textbf{Adjusted FY25 EBITDA of \$18.4M} (a net positive addback of \textbf{+\$4.2M} over reported EBITDA of \$14.2M). Historical revenue grew at a 22.4\% CAGR (FY23--FY25). Key deal risks include \textbf{31.2\% customer concentration} across the top 3 accounts and \textbf{\$1.8M/yr of capitalized R\&D software costs} that require ongoing operational expense treatment. We recommend executing the transaction with an adjusted Purchase Price incorporating a \textbf{\$12.8M Net Debt deduction} and a \textbf{\$8.5M Working Capital Target Peg}.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection*{Detailed Rationale for Primary QoE Adjustments}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.5pt, parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Out-of-Period Revenue Alignment (\$-2.40M in FY25):} Synapse recognized multi-year cloud contract fees upfront upon signing under legacy ASC 605 rules. Under ASC 606 ratable principles, \$2.4M of FY25 revenue belongs to FY26 contract periods.
  \item \textbf{Founder Excess Compensation (+\$1.30M in FY25):} Founding executives drew \$2.5M in annual compensation. Executive search benchmarking establishes replacement executive compensation at \$1.2M annually, creating a recurring \$1.3M cost reduction.
  \item \textbf{Capitalized R\&D Software Reversal (\$-1.80M in FY25):} Management capitalized 65\% of software engineering payroll. Audit testing determined 40\% of this work involves maintenance and bug fixes, requiring ongoing OpEx treatment.
\end{enumerate}
