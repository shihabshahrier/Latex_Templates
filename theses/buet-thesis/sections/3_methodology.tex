\chapter{METHODOLOGY}
\label{ch:methodology}

Our proposed architecture integrates a bidirectional LSTM layer with a multi-head self-attention block, allowing the network to capture both local structure and global context.

\section{System Architecture}
The system layout is depicted in the standard layers:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **Input Embedding**: Maps characters $\{A, C, G, T\}$ into a dense vector space.
    \item **BiLSTM Layer**: Processes sequence in both forward and backward directions.
    \item **Multi-Head Attention**: Captures semantic alignments across long ranges.
\end{enumerate}
