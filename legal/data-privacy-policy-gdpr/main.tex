\section*{\color{navyink}Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction}

Nexa Technologies B.\,V.\ (``\textbf{Nexa}'', ``\textbf{we}'', ``\textbf{us}'', or ``\textbf{our}'') provides
web-based document collaboration and workflow software to organisations across the European
Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and beyond. This Data Privacy Policy \& GDPR
Compliance Terms document (the ``\textbf{Policy}'') explains, in plain language backed by
precise legal references, how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal data when
you visit \texttt{www.nexatech.example}, use the Nexa application, or otherwise interact with us
as a customer, prospective customer, website visitor, or employee of a customer organisation.

This Policy is issued in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the \textbf{General Data
Protection Regulation}, ``GDPR''), the UK GDPR as retained in domestic law, and applicable
member-state implementing legislation. Where Nexa acts as a \textbf{data processor} on behalf of
a customer (for example, processing content your organisation uploads to the platform), the
terms of the separate Data Processing Addendum (DPA) govern that processing; this Policy
addresses Nexa's role as a \textbf{data controller} for account, billing, marketing, and
website-visitor data.

\begin{keybox}[At a glance]
\begin{itemize}
  \item We are the \textbf{data controller} for account, billing, and marketing data, and a
        \textbf{data processor} for customer content stored in the platform.
  \item Our lead supervisory authority is the \textbf{Dutch Data Protection Authority}
        (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens), as our main establishment is in Amsterdam.
  \item You may exercise your GDPR rights at any time by contacting our Data Protection
        Officer at \texttt{dpo@nexatech.example}.
\end{itemize}
\end{keybox}

\section{International Data Transfers}

Nexa's primary infrastructure is hosted within the European Economic Area. Where a transfer of
personal data outside the EEA is necessary --- for example, to a sub-processor providing
customer support tooling with servers in the United States --- we ensure an adequate level of
protection through one or more of the following safeguards:

\begin{sloppypar}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)} adopted by the European Commission
        (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), incorporated into every applicable
        sub-processor agreement;
  \item \textbf{Adequacy decisions}, where the destination country is recognised by the
        European Commission as providing adequate protection;
  \item \textbf{Supplementary technical measures}, including encryption in transit and at
        rest, and pseudonymisation of analytics identifiers.
\end{itemize}
\end{sloppypar}

A current, version-controlled list of sub-processors, their processing location, and applicable
transfer mechanism is published at \url{nexatech.example/subprocessors} and updated at least
30 days before any new sub-processor is engaged, in accordance with our DPA notification
obligations.

\section{Data Breach Notification}

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms,
Nexa will:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Notify the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens without undue delay, and in any event within
        \textbf{72 hours} of becoming aware of the breach, as required by Art.\ 33 GDPR;
  \item Notify affected data subjects directly, without undue delay, where the breach is
        likely to result in a \textit{high} risk to their rights and freedoms (Art.\ 34
        GDPR);
  \item Document the breach, its effects, and remedial action taken in our internal breach
        register, made available to supervisory authorities upon request.
\end{enumerate}
