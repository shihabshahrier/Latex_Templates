\section{Term and Termination}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\textbf{10.1\enspace Expiration.}\enspace This Agreement expires at the end of the Term unless
renewed by mutual written agreement not less than ninety (90)~days prior to expiration.

\textbf{10.2\enspace Termination for Cause.}\enspace Either party may terminate this Agreement
by written notice if: (a)~the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to
cure within sixty (60)~days of written notice; or (b)~the other party becomes insolvent,
makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to insolvency proceedings.

\textbf{10.3\enspace Termination for Patent Challenge.}\enspace Licensor may terminate this
Agreement immediately upon written notice if Licensee or any Affiliate directly or indirectly
challenges the validity, enforceability, or ownership of any Licensed Patent.

\textbf{10.4\enspace Effect of Termination.}\enspace Upon expiration or termination:
(a)~all licenses granted herein immediately cease; (b)~Licensee shall cease all use and
distribution of the Licensed IP within ninety (90)~days; (c)~each party shall return or
certifiably destroy the other's confidential information; and (d)~all accrued payment
obligations survive.
