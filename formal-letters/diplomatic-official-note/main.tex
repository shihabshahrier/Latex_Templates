Official Passports; Establishment of a Joint Cultural Heritage Commission}

\opening{The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Meridian presents its
compliments to the Embassy of the Kingdom of Solvenia and has the honour to
refer to the exchanges between the two Governments concerning the
strengthening of bilateral relations in the fields of consular facilitation
and cultural cooperation.}

In this connection, the Ministry has the further honour to communicate the
following to the Embassy:

\begin{notepara}

\item The Ministry recalls the proceedings of the Ninth Session of the
Meridian--Solvenia Joint Bilateral Commission, held in Cardessa from 3 to 5
June 2026, at which the respective delegations reached understanding on two
matters of immediate practical benefit to the nationals of both States: the
reciprocal facilitation of entry for holders of diplomatic and official
passports, and the establishment of a standing mechanism for cooperation on
shared cultural heritage.

\item The Government of the Republic of Meridian is pleased to confirm its
agreement to waive, on a strictly reciprocal basis, the requirement of an
entry visa for holders of valid diplomatic or official/service passports
issued by the Kingdom of Solvenia, in respect of stays not exceeding ninety
(90) days within any period of one hundred and eighty (180) days, for
purposes not involving remunerated activity. The particulars of this
arrangement, including the treatment of accredited diplomatic couriers and
accompanying family members, are set out in \textbf{Annex I} to this Note.

\item The Ministry further proposes the establishment of a Joint Cultural
Heritage Commission, composed of an equal number of representatives
designated by each Government, whose function shall be to identify, and
recommend measures for the conservation, restoration, and reciprocal
exhibition of, moveable and immoveable cultural property of shared or
comparable historical significance situated within the territory of either
State. A draft Protocol setting out the composition, functions, and working
methods of the proposed Commission is enclosed as \textbf{Annex II}.

\item Should the foregoing meet with the concurrence of the Government of
the Kingdom of Solvenia, the Ministry has the honour to propose that the
present Note, together with the Embassy's Note in reply expressing such
concurrence, shall constitute an Agreement between the two Governments,
which shall enter into force thirty (30) days after the date of the
Embassy's reply and shall remain in force until terminated by either Party
upon ninety (90) days' written notice through the diplomatic channel.

\item The competent authorities of the Republic of Meridian, namely the
Directorate-General of Migration and the National Heritage Institute, stand
ready to enter into the technical arrangements necessary to give practical
effect to the foregoing at the earliest convenience of the Embassy.

\item The Ministry avails itself of this opportunity to request that the
Embassy transmit the substance of this Note to the Government of the Kingdom
of Solvenia at its earliest convenience, and would welcome confirmation of
receipt through the usual channel.

\end{notepara}

\closing{The Ministry of Foreign Affairs avails itself of this opportunity
to renew to the Embassy of the Kingdom of Solvenia the assurances of its
highest consideration.}

\AnnexHeader{Annex II}{Draft Protocol on the Joint Cultural Heritage Commission}

\noindent The Government of the Republic of Meridian and the Government of
the Kingdom of Solvenia (\textquotedblleft the Parties\textquotedblright),

\smallskip
\noindent Desiring to safeguard and present to future generations the
cultural heritage held in common or of comparable significance to both
peoples, and to deepen cooperation between their competent institutions,

\smallskip
\noindent Have agreed as follows:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\color{noteblue}Article \arabic*.},
  leftmargin=2.4cm,itemsep=8pt,topsep=6pt]

\item \textbf{Establishment and Purpose.} There is hereby established a
Joint Cultural Heritage Commission (\textquotedblleft the
Commission\textquotedblright), which shall serve as the principal
consultative body between the Parties on matters of shared cultural
heritage, including conservation, restoration, provenance research, and
reciprocal exhibition.

\item \textbf{Composition.} The Commission shall consist of six members,
three designated by each Party, drawn from the respective national heritage
institute, ministry of culture, and academic sector. Each Party shall
designate a Co-Chair from among its members.

\item \textbf{Functions.} The Commission shall: (a) identify sites,
collections, and objects of shared or comparable significance; (b) recommend
joint conservation and restoration projects and their funding arrangements;
(c) facilitate the temporary reciprocal loan of objects for exhibition; and
(d) report annually to both Governments through the diplomatic channel.

\item \textbf{Meetings.} The Commission shall meet in ordinary session once
each calendar year, alternately in Cardessa and in the capital of the
Kingdom of Solvenia, and in extraordinary session upon the written request
of either Co-Chair.

\item \textbf{Funding.} Each Party shall bear the expenses of its own
delegation's participation in meetings of the Commission. Costs of joint
projects recommended by the Commission shall be apportioned as agreed
project-by-project and recorded in a separate implementing arrangement.

\item \textbf{Entry into Force and Duration.} This Protocol shall enter into
force on the date specified in paragraph 4 of the Note to which it is
annexed and shall remain in force for so long as that Note, as accepted,
remains in effect.

\end{enumerate}
