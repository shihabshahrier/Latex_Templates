\opening{Dear Ms. Warrick,}

Thank you for the productive working session your team hosted on July 9th at Nexa Analytics' Cambridge office. Following our discussion with you, Mr. Okonkwo, and the platform engineering group, Meridian Consulting Group is pleased to submit this letter as a follow-up summary of the proposed engagement scope, revised timeline, and investment for the Data Governance \& Reporting Modernization initiative, together with the enclosed draft Statement of Work for your review.

As we discussed, Nexa Analytics' current reporting stack has outgrown the manual reconciliation processes that were adequate at an earlier stage of growth. Our recommended approach addresses this in four sequential phases, each concluding with a formal deliverable review with your leadership team before work proceeds to the next stage.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.9cm}p{2.5cm}p{5.3cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Timeline} & \textbf{Key Deliverable} \\
\midrule
1. Discovery & Weeks 1--3 & Current-state data lineage map and governance gap assessment \\
2. Design & Weeks 4--7 & Target-state architecture and reporting taxonomy \\
3. Implementation & Weeks 8--14 & Deployed governance controls and automated reporting pipeline \\
4. Change \& Handover & Weeks 15--16 & Trained internal owners and closeout documentation \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.4cm}

This represents a modest compression of the sixteen-week estimate we shared verbally, achieved by running the data-lineage discovery workstream in parallel with early design activities rather than sequentially, an approach we have used successfully with similarly sized organizations in the financial services and healthcare analytics sectors.

Our methodology emphasizes embedded knowledge transfer over external dependency: at each phase, a designated member of your data team is paired with a Meridian consultant so that operational ownership of the new controls sits with Nexa Analytics, not with us, by the time the engagement concludes. We have found this pairing model to be the single strongest predictor of whether a governance program survives past the first two fiscal quarters.

\pagebreak

The engagement would be staffed by three consultants, each of whom has led comparable modernization work in your sector:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt,topsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Priya Natarajan}, Lead Data Architect --- twelve years designing governed reporting platforms for mid-market analytics firms.
  \item \textbf{Marcus Chen}, Senior Change Management Advisor --- specializes in operational handover and internal capability building.
  \item \textbf{Sofia Delgado}, Analytics Engineer --- will lead pipeline implementation and automated testing of the new reporting layer.
\end{itemize}

Detailed biographies for each team member are included in the enclosed materials.
