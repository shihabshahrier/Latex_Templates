\sectionrule
{\sffamily\bfseries\color{NavyDeep}\normalsize I.\enspace FACTUAL BACKGROUND}
\sectionrule

On or about \textbf{March 1, 2025}, Nexa Capital Holdings, LLC and Synapse Technologies, Inc. (\textquotedblleft\textbf{Synapse}\textquotedblright{} or \textquotedblleft\textbf{Respondent}\textquotedblright) entered into that certain \textit{Software Development \& Services Agreement} (the \textquotedblleft\textbf{Agreement}\textquotedblright), bearing contract number \textbf{SYN-NXA-2025-003}. Under the Agreement, Synapse agreed to design, develop, test, and deliver a proprietary enterprise resource planning platform (the \textquotedblleft\textbf{Platform}\textquotedblright) for Nexa's internal operations, in exchange for total consideration of \textbf{\$1,150,000.00}.

Nexa performed all of its obligations under the Agreement, including timely payment of two milestone installments totaling \textbf{\$600,000.00} on March 15, 2025 (\$250,000) and May 30, 2025 (\$350,000). Despite receiving substantial payment, Synapse has:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Failed to deliver the Phase II deliverables by the contractual deadline of June 30, 2025;
  \item Delivered non-conforming beta code that failed Nexa's acceptance testing on July 8, 2025 and July 15, 2025;
  \item Refused to provide a remediation timeline despite written requests dated July 10 and July 17, 2025;
  \item Diverted key development personnel (\textit{see} Exhibit A) to competing client projects in breach of the exclusivity clause at \S 7.4 of the Agreement;
  \item Failed to maintain required insurance coverage as mandated by \S 12.2 of the Agreement.
\end{itemize}

These acts and omissions constitute a \textbf{material breach} of the Agreement, entitling Nexa to damages and other relief as further described herein.

\vspace{4pt}
\sectionrule
{\sffamily\bfseries\color{NavyDeep}\normalsize II.\enspace CLAIMS ASSERTED}
\sectionrule

\begin{tcolorbox}[demandstyle={Claim 1 \ \ Breach of Contract}]
\noindent Synapse's failure to deliver conforming deliverables by the agreed milestones, and its continued failure to cure despite demand, constitutes a material breach of the Agreement. Nexa is entitled to recover all direct and consequential damages flowing from such breach, including without limitation the amounts paid for undelivered or non-conforming work and costs incurred in engaging substitute vendors.

\medskip
\noindent\claimlabel{Governing Law:} Illinois Uniform Commercial Code, 810 ILCS 5/2-601 et seq.; common law contract.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[demandstyle={Claim 2 \ \ Unjust Enrichment \& Restitution}]
\noindent In the alternative, Synapse has been unjustly enriched by retaining \$600,000.00 of Nexa's funds without delivering the bargained-for performance. Nexa seeks full restitution of all amounts paid under the Agreement to the extent not offset by the fair market value of any conforming work product actually delivered and accepted.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[demandstyle={Claim 3 \ \ Breach of Implied Covenant of Good Faith \& Fair Dealing}]
\noindent Synapse's concealment of personnel reassignments, its failure to disclose known deficiencies in its development capacity, and its repeated refusal to engage in remediation discussions constitute a breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing implied in every Illinois contract.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[alertstyle={Claim 4 \ \ Fraudulent Misrepresentation (Potential)}]
\noindent Evidence gathered to date suggests that Synapse represented to Nexa, at the time of contracting, that it had sufficient staffing and technical capacity to complete the Platform -- representations Synapse knew or should have known to be false. Meridian Legal Associates reserves the right to assert fraud claims, which may support punitive damages and fee-shifting, upon completion of pre-litigation discovery.

\medskip
\noindent\claimlabel{Note:} This claim will be formally evaluated upon receipt of Synapse's response and supporting documentation.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=4pt,
    label=\protect\demandnum{\arabic*}]
  \item \textbf{Payment:} Remit to Meridian Legal Associates, P.C.   \item \textbf{Delivery or Source Code Escrow:} Either (a) deliver fully conforming Phase II deliverables meeting the acceptance criteria in Exhibit B of the Agreement, or (b) deposit all source code, documentation, and development assets into a mutually agreed escrow pending resolution, so that Nexa may engage a third-party developer to complete the Platform.

  \item \textbf{Document Preservation:} Immediately implement and maintain a litigation hold on all documents, communications (including Slack, email, and project management tools), contracts, billing records, and personnel records related to Nexa, the Platform, and any concurrent client projects.   \item \textbf{Written Response:} Provide a written response from counsel admitted to practice in the relevant jurisdiction, addressing each claim and demand herein, no later than the Response Deadline. Silence will be construed as an admission of the material facts herein.

  \item \textbf{Insurance Documentation:} Provide certificates of insurance demonstrating maintenance of coverage required by \S 12.2 of the Agreement for all periods from the effective date, or documentation of any coverage gaps and the steps taken to remedy them.
