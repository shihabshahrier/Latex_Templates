\opening{Dear Dr.~Ionescu,}

On behalf of the Department of Computer Science \& Engineering and the Faculty Appointments
Committee, it is my pleasure to confirm, in writing, the terms of your appointment as
Assistant Professor on the tenure track, following the vote of the department faculty on
July 21, 2026 and subsequent approval by the Dean of the Meridian College of Engineering.
This letter, together with its enclosures, constitutes the department's official record of
the appointment and supersedes the verbal offer extended on July 24, 2026.

We were especially persuaded by the trajectory of your work on fault-tolerant consensus
protocols and by the letters from your referees describing your effectiveness in the
classroom. The Committee's recommendation was unanimous, and I am glad to have the chance to
put that enthusiasm into an official record.

\begin{center}
\begin{tcolorbox}[meridianbox, width=0.92\textwidth, title=Key Terms of Appointment]
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rank:} Assistant Professor, tenure-track
  \item \textbf{Effective date:} September 1, 2026
  \item \textbf{Base academic-year salary:} \$96,500, paid over 24 installments
  \item \textbf{Tenure clock:} six-year probationary period; mandatory third-year review in
        Spring 2029 and tenure review in Spring 2032
  \item \textbf{Startup research fund:} \$45,000, disbursed over three years and administered
        through the Vertex Grants Portal
  \item \textbf{Reporting line:} Chair, Department of Computer Science \& Engineering
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{center}

In recognition of the laboratory build-out described in your proposal, the department has
approved a standard 2/2 teaching load reduced to 1/1 (a 50\% teaching release) for your first
two academic years. The confirmed assignments for your inaugural year are summarized below.

Your service assignments for the coming academic year, agreed with the Committee on
Committees, are as follows.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Graduate Admissions Committee} --- member, reviewing PhD and MS applications
        for the 2027 entering cohort
  \item \textbf{Curriculum \& Accreditation Committee} --- member, supporting the department's
        current ABET self-study cycle
  \item \textbf{Distributed Systems Laboratory} --- co-principal-investigator liaison for the
        department's sponsored research agreement with Nexa Research Institute, covering
        replicated-log infrastructure for the lab's testbed cluster
\end{itemize}

On the matter of facilities: you will be assigned office 318 in Innovation Hall and shared
bench space in the Innovation Hall B-Wing alongside the Concurrent Systems Group, effective
from your start date. Compute credits for large-scale experiments are available immediately
through the department's standing partnership with Apex Cloud Platforms; the onboarding
packet enclosed with this letter explains how to request an allocation. Payroll, benefits
enrollment, and tax-withholding forms are processed through the Synapse HR Portal, and you
will receive system credentials under separate cover from Human Resources during orientation
week.

New Faculty Orientation runs from August 25 through August 27, 2026 in Bellcourt Auditorium
and is mandatory for all incoming tenure-track faculty. A detailed agenda, including sessions
on IRB procedures, library research services, and the department's mentoring program, will
follow by email no later than August 10, 2026. I have asked Professor Daniel Achterberg, your
assigned faculty mentor for the coming year, to reach out directly to arrange an introductory
meeting before classes begin.

Please countersign the enclosed appointment summary and return one copy to the department
office by August 15, 2026 to formalize this agreement. Should any of the terms above differ
from your understanding of the offer, contact my office immediately so that we can resolve
the discrepancy before your start date.

On behalf of the entire department, welcome to Meridian University. We are genuinely glad you
are joining us and look forward to the contribution your research and teaching will make to
the department in the years ahead.
